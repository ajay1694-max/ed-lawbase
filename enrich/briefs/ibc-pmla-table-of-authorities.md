**How to read this table.** Every decision the Wazalwar *IBC-PMLA Compendium* (2027 ed.) analyses in full is listed alphabetically. The first column names it; the court and date, citation and the compendium's status note follow. "Point" is our one-line summary. Where a decision is in LawBase, the last column gives its case_id. Those decisions have a headnote checked against the judgment itself, and **the headnote controls where it differs from the compendium**. The check found the compendium wrong on *Nitin Jain*, *Rajiv Chakraborty*, *Pavana Dibbur*, *P. Mohanraj*, *National Spot Exchange*, *Swiss Ribbons* and *Bikram Chatterji*. Citations or dates marked [?] were illegible in the source and are unverified. NCLT and NCLAT orders are mostly not in LawBase; obtain them from the NCLT/NCLAT websites before citing. Book chapters (Ch.) are given so that the topic briefs (see `ibc-00-overview-start-here`) can be followed.
