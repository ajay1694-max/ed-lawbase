**Bikram Chatterji & Ors. v. Union of India & Ors.**, [2019] 9 SCR 289 : 2019 INSC 799, W.P.(C) No. 940 of 2017 (Arun Mishra and Uday Umesh Lalit, JJ.; 23.07.2019).

**Context.** Amrapali group companies had taken money from homebuyers for about 42,000 flats. Bank of Baroda's s.7 IBC petition against one group company had been admitted, and a moratorium was in place (para 6). The homebuyers came directly to the Supreme Court. The Court ordered a forensic audit and froze and attached properties of directors and group companies.

**Held.**
- **Paras 142-147.** The Court accepted the forensic audit. Homebuyers' money was diverted, including about Rs. 2,996.20 crore. The diversion was done through dummy companies, bogus bills, undervalued sales of flats, and an investment from J.P. Morgan that broke FEMA and FDI norms. "The money laundering was resorted to by Amrapali Group/ Directors." (para 147).
- **Paras 148-150.** RERA registrations were cancelled, and the lease deeds were cancelled with the lessee's rights vested in the Court Receiver. No valid mortgage had been created in favour of the banks. The dues of the Noida and Greater Noida authorities and of the banks are to be recovered from other properties attached by the Court, not from homebuyers or the projects.
- **Para 151.** "For violations of FEMA and FDI norms, we direct the Enforcement Directorate to make investigation in accordance with the law and submit reports quarterly to this Court. Money laundering aspect is also to be looked into by concerned authorities."
- **Para 154 (operative directions).**
  - (iv) NBCC is to complete the projects.
  - (vi) ED and the concerned authorities are to investigate and fix liability for the FEMA violations, fraud and money laundering.
  - (x) Shri R. Venkataramani is appointed Court Receiver.

**IBC-PMLA use.**
- This is an example of the Supreme Court putting victims' recovery ahead of the creditor waterfall where homebuyers' money had been diverted. The Court directed ED to investigate and treated attached diverted assets as the source of recovery.
- It supports the argument that money diverted from homebuyers or depositors is not a free asset for secured creditors (paras 150 and 153).
- It is an exceptional remedy given in the Supreme Court's own writ jurisdiction. It is not a template for routine CIRPs.
- The judgment did not itself direct ED to register cases or make PMLA attachments. The attachments mentioned were made by the Court. Plead paras 151 and 154(vi) as they are worded.

**Later history (per compendium).** The Court continued to supervise the matter through further orders on forensic tracing, construction finance from a consortium led by Bank of Baroda, the SBICAP stressed-real-estate fund, and transfer of homebuyer complaints. None of these orders was checked in the corpus.
