**Smt. Lakshmi Anil Salgaocar v. Commissioner of Customs (Appeals), Mumbai-I**, FPA-FE-24/GOA/2024, 02.09.2026. Appellate Tribunal under SAFEMA, New Delhi. Justice Munishwar Nath Bhandari (Chairman), V. Anandarajan and Balesh Kumar (Members).

**FEMA — sections 4 and 37A — continued holding of foreign exchange — equivalent-value seizure — retrospectivity.** The Tribunal dismissed an appeal by the administratrix of Anil Salgaocar's estate against confirmation of seizure of shares in 33 Indian companies. On the material concerning beneficial ownership of overseas companies, foreign trading profits and the Singapore litigation, it found a sufficient basis for the Directorate's suspicion that foreign exchange continued to be held abroad contrary to section 4 (paras 11–13). It treated seizure under section 37A as a temporary protective measure, with further action dependent on adjudication (para 14). The Tribunal found no retrospectivity problem on these facts because the estate's acquisition and continued overseas holding followed the Singapore judgment of 28.02.2023; alternatively, even treating the profits as accruing in 2004–2012, they continued to be held when section 37A commenced on 09.09.2015 (paras 15–16). It rejected the residency objections on the evidence and the administratrix's representative capacity (para 17). The confirmation order dated 29.12.2023 and seizure order dated 08.08.2023 were upheld (para 18).

**Limits:** This is a three-member tribunal bench. The operative reasoning does not expressly overrule Ashwani Kumar Mehra or state that it decides a formal reference on conflicting views. The original contains inconsistent seized-value figures, so no exact amount is adopted here. Residency reasoning is evidence-specific and includes the statutory intention/employment exceptions (para 17).
