**Rohit Vij v. Directorate of Enforcement**, Delhi High Court, Purushaindra Kumar Kaurav J., 10.08.2026. BAIL APPLN. 4461/2025 & CRL.M.(BAIL) 490/2026; **2026:DHC:6498**, visually verified on the original PDF first page.

In this bail proceeding, the Court distinguished merits-based termination of a predicate case from compromise-based quashing (paras 15-20). It noted disputed coercion surrounding the compromise and 24 further FIRs added to the ECIR; the propriety of those inclusions remained open for trial (paras 19-22). The Court assessed the wider alleged laundering scheme rather than restricting proceeds to the original complainant's loss (paras 25-27), rejected parity on the applicant's distinct alleged role (para 28), and refused bail on the material concerning involvement, non-disclosure and flight risk under section 45 (paras 30-39 and 49-57). These are bail-stage findings, not a determination of guilt, and do not establish that PMLA proceedings survive every form of predicate quashing.
