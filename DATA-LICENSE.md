# Data license

The judgments and statutes distributed as Releases from this repository (`lawbase-data-*.zip`) are
built from **Open India Law** by Vaquill AI (https://github.com/Vaquill-AI/open-india-law), snapshot
v2026.08.1, licensed **CC BY 4.0**, plus 45 judgments individually downloaded from the Directorate of
Enforcement's own public portal (enforcementdirectorate.gov.in/acts-and-rules/important-judgements/) -
government judicial records, reproducible under s.52(1)(q) of the Copyright Act 1957.

Headnotes, issue tags and research briefs added under `enrich/` in this repository are original work
of ED LawBase contributors, also CC BY 4.0.

Attribution when reusing this data: "Open India Law by Vaquill AI (CC BY 4.0)" plus this repository.

This is a research aid. Always verify against the certified copy / SCR / SCC before relying on or
citing any judgment.
